## Retirement policy

Only obsolete root eval locations, the custom portable field spelling, duplicated projection definitions, and proven-redundant orchestration are retirement candidates. Runtime/provider/home controls, controllers, fixture oracles, campaign durability, human/source grading protocols, historical readers with consumers, and installed-artifact tests remain.

Do not delete an old safety test in the same change that first introduces its replacement. First demonstrate equivalent interface coverage and independently review gaps. Direct Hadden execution is retained while a platform/consumer remains unqualified. When paths move, update all live in-repository consumers in one change; an additional old-path entry point needs a specific owner-approved compatibility exception, not a relabeling as an adapter.
