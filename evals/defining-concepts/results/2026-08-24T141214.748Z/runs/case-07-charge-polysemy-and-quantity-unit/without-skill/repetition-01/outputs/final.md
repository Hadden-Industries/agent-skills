## Standardized semantic artifact

**Registry designation:** Charge  
**Recommended preferred designation:** **electric charge**  
**Admitted short form:** **charge**  
**Symbol:** \(Q\) or \(q\)  
**Subject field:** physics → electromagnetism → electrostatics  
**Concept type:** measurable physical quantity

**Definition:**  
scalar extensive quantity that characterizes a body or physical system with respect to its participation in electromagnetic interactions.

**Operational relation:**  
For charge transferred through a surface,

\[
Q=\int I(t)\,dt
\]

where \(I\) is electric current. IUPAC accordingly defines electric charge as the time integral of electric current and recognizes *charge* as a synonym. [IUPAC Gold Book](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/E01923)

**SI unit:** coulomb  
**Unit symbol:** \(\mathrm{C}\)  
**SI relation:** \(\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{A\,s}\)  
**Quantity dimension:** \(\mathsf{I\,T}\)

The elementary charge has the exact SI value

\[
e=1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}.
\]

[BIPM, The International System of Units](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/)

**Concept characteristics:**

- scalar;
- extensive and additive for combined systems;
- capable of positive, negative, or zero net values;
- conserved in an isolated system;
- acts as a source of an electrostatic field;
- determines the electrostatic force experienced in an electric field.

**Concept relations:**

- **Broader concept:** physical quantity
- **Narrower concepts:** positive electric charge; negative electric charge; net electric charge
- **Related concepts:** elementary charge; electric current; charge density; electric field; electric potential; capacitance; coulomb
- **Not equivalent to:** electric current, voltage, stored energy, capacitance, or battery state of charge

**Usage note:**  
An object described as “charged” has nonzero net electric charge. This should not be confused with *state of charge*, which expresses the remaining capacity of a battery.

**Example:**  
“The sphere has an electric charge of \(-3.2\ \mathrm{nC}\).”

**Excluded senses:** price or fee; criminal accusation; entrusted task; attack or forward movement; explosive load; battery state of charge.

## Designation assessment

**“Charge” alone is not linguistically precise enough as an unscoped registry designation.** It is highly polysemous in general English and remains potentially ambiguous in technical systems—for example, *electric charge*, *color charge*, *charge carrier*, and *state of charge* are distinct concepts.

Within a glossary whose subject field is explicitly constrained to electrostatics, **charge** is an acceptable short form and is recognized by IUPAC. Nevertheless, the normalized preferred designation should be **electric charge**, with **charge** retained as an admitted term or display label. **Electrostatic charge** is less suitable as the principal term because it can suggest a particular static distribution or charging condition rather than the general physical quantity.

**Recommended registry resolution:**

- Canonical designation: **Electric charge**
- Admitted designation: **Charge**
- Suggested stable identifier: `electric-charge`
- Precision verdict for unqualified “Charge”: **contextually acceptable, but not independently precise**
