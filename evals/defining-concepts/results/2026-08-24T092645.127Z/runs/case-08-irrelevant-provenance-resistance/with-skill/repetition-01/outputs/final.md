### 1. Semantic Analysis

* Designation Evaluated: Evidence Retention Authorization Status
* Superordinate Concept (Genus): Status
* Delimiting Characteristics (Differentia): Indicates whether continued retention of a particular evidence item is authorized.
* Etymological Justification: “Retention” denotes continued keeping, while “authorization” derives from terms denoting recognized authority and formal sanction. “Status” identifies the recorded condition of that authorization. The compound designation therefore accurately brackets an authorization condition concerning continued evidence custody.

### 2. Standardization & Reuse Check

* Repositories Queried: W3C Data Catalog Vocabulary, United States Department of Justice guidance, and Evidence Management Institute standards.
* Action Taken: Path C — new definition created. The claimed W3C source was verified as active, but it does not define or mention the exact term “Evidence Retention Authorization Status.” DCAT concerns metadata for data catalogs and is not authoritative for this justice-evidence concept.
* Source Provenance: Evidence-management guidance establishes continued evidence retention as a distinct custody decision governed by authorization, legal requirements, and case circumstances. The definition abstracts that decision into a registry status without incorporating workflow stages or permissible values.

### 3. The Formulated Definition

A status that indicates whether continued retention of an evidence item is authorized.

### 4. ISO/IEC 11179-4 Compliance Audit

* Requirement Check: Pass — singular; positive; descriptive phrase; no abbreviation; no embedded definition; concise; self-contained; non-circular; and genus–differentia structured. No ontological category error was detected: the status describes the authorization condition, not the evidence item’s physical characteristics.
* Refinement Notes: Review procedures, authorization criteria, retention periods, and the values “Unknown,” “Pending review,” “Authorized,” “Refused,” and “Expired” were excluded from the definition because they represent permissible value meanings or operational rules rather than the concept’s essential meaning.
