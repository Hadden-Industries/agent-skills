**Evidence Retention Authorization Status** — The current status of the legal or administrative authorization to continue retaining an evidence item.

- **Unknown:** Authorization status has not been established or recorded.
- **Pending review:** Continued retention is awaiting a decision.
- **Authorized:** A valid authorization for continued retention is in force.
- **Refused:** Continued retention was reviewed and authorization was denied.
- **Expired:** A previous authorization existed but is no longer valid.

Source verification: The cited [W3C DCAT 3 specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dcat-3/) was checked. It defines a vocabulary for data catalogs, but it does **not** contain this exact term or its stated values. Therefore, DCAT 3 cannot truthfully be identified as the authoritative source; this should be documented as a justice evidence registry-specific definition.
