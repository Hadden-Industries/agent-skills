**Evidence Retention Authorization Status**: The current state of the decision determining whether an evidence item may continue to be retained under the applicable legal or administrative authority.

- **Unknown** — The authorization status cannot be determined or has not been recorded.
- **Pending review** — Continued retention is awaiting a decision.
- **Authorized** — Continued retention is currently approved.
- **Refused** — Authorization for continued retention was denied.
- **Expired** — A previous authorization existed but is no longer valid.

**Source verification:** I verified the cited [W3C DCAT 3 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dcat-3/). It does **not** define “Evidence Retention Authorization Status,” evidence retention, or this value set. DCAT is a general vocabulary for describing data catalogs and permits domain-specific metadata extensions. Therefore, the definition above is a proposed justice-registry definition, and DCAT 3 should not be cited as its authoritative source.
